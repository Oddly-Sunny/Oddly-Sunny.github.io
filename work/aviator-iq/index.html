About Aviator IQ

FAA knowledge test prep for every airman certificate.

Aviation exams have a reputation: huge handbooks, cryptic charts, and prep tools that look like they were built in 2004. We thought that was a very fixable problem.

Aviator IQ covers 13 FAA knowledge exams, from Private Pilot to ATP. Every question is mapped to the source it comes from, and the figures you'll see on test day (yes, the sectional charts) sit right next to the question.

Then there's the part we're proudest of: an IQ Readiness Score that blends coverage, accuracy, recall and speed, so you know when you're ready instead of guessing.
