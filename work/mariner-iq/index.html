About Mariner IQ

Pass your Coast Guard captain's license exam.

Getting a captain's licence is a serious goal, and the prep options mostly feel like a flea market. Mariner IQ is the clean, calm alternative.

It's built from the Coast Guard's own sample exams, covering six-pack, 100-ton master, able seaman and other credentials, with a plain-English explanation on every single answer.

And because we like people, it's pay once, no subscription. There's even a night mode that turns the screen bridge-red for the wheelhouse.
