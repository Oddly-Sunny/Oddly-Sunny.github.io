About Insurance Pass

Pass your insurance license exam with practice built on your state's law.

Insurance exams are state-by-state, and the rules really do change when you cross a border. Most prep tools wave at that. We went and read the law.

Insurance Pass covers four lines of authority across the states and DC. Questions are built from state statutes and regulations, and each answer shows you the provision behind it.

It's licence prep that treats you like a future professional: no trick questions, no hand-waving, just the rule and why it's the rule.
