About

An AI EdTech company for the exams that open doors.

Oddly Sunny builds AI-powered exam prep for licensing and certification, from driving and aviation tests to insurance, radio, maritime and citizenship exams.

What we build

Oddly Sunny is an educational technology company. We build AI-powered study products for the high-stakes exams people take to drive, fly, sell insurance, operate a radio, captain a vessel or become a citizen.

These exams are written by government agencies and licensing bodies, and the official material is dense. We turn it into practice that is personal, accurate and easier to stay with, so learners know what the test covers and when they are ready to take it.

The content engine

Our products share one pipeline and one standard: nothing reaches a learner unless it can be traced to an official source. The pipeline turns statutes and agency handbooks into question banks, checks them, and keeps them current.

Direct source grounding

Every question carries a word-for-word quote from the official manual or regulation it came from. No source, no question.

Independent blind check

A second model answers questions without seeing the key. When it disagrees, the question goes back for review.

Official exam blueprint

Question counts, time limits and pass marks match the real exam, and topic coverage is checked against the published outline.

Giveaway detection

We test banks for answer patterns, like the right answer sitting in one position, and fix them so scores reflect knowledge.

Edition monitoring

We watch the official sources and flag new editions, so nobody studies yesterday's rules.

Readiness scoring

A readiness score that blends coverage, accuracy, recall and speed, measured against the official pass mark.

The AI layer

On top of the engine sits Sunny, our AI study coach. It is built for education from the ground up: it looks answers up in the official source instead of making them up.

A closed library of official sources

Sunny retrieves from the official material behind each exam, from state handbooks to federal regulations, and answers from that text.

An agent that uses tools

Sunny searches the handbook, pulls practice questions, starts quizzes and checks progress, then answers with the page it used.

Numbers checked before they show

Every figure in an answer, from a speed limit to a fee, is checked against the source first. When the source is silent, Sunny says so.

A model of each learner

Sunny knows the learner's test, date, strong topics and weak spots, so each session starts from that picture and not from zero.

One engine, every exam, localized

A single domain-neutral engine powers every brand. Each plugs in its own sources, rules and question bank, and learners can study in English, Spanish, Chinese or French.

Built to scale to new exams

Ingestion and delivery are separate, reusable layers, so a new exam or jurisdiction builds on what already works.

Seven products, one engine

Each product is written for its own exam and market, and all of them run on the same platform.
