Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DMV IQ ("the App," "we," or "us") handles information when you use the App.

DMV IQ is a driving test preparation app that helps users study for their state DMV written knowledge test. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed to be used without creating an account and without collecting personal information, except where necessary to operate and improve the App.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

We do not require you to submit personal information to use the App.

If you contact us via the in-app contact form, we may receive:

  • Your name and email address
  • The content of your message
  • Device type and app version (automatically attached)

This information is used only to respond to your inquiry.

2. Local Data

The App stores study progress, preferences, and settings locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your selected state and vehicle type
  • Study progress and spaced repetition data
  • Test results and IQ score history
  • App settings (dark mode, notification preferences, etc.)

This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Automatically Collected Information

The App may collect limited, non-identifying information through third-party services:

  • App usage events (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports and performance data (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Device type and operating system

This data is used solely to improve app stability and user experience.

How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Operate and maintain the App
  • Improve functionality and performance
  • Respond to user inquiries
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the study experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • EmailJS — contact form delivery

Data Sharing

We do not share personal information except:

  • With service providers that support app functionality (analytics, crash reporting, subscriptions)
  • When required by law

All such providers are required to handle data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it

Data Retention

  • Local data is stored on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing app data
  • Analytics and crash data is retained according to the respective provider policies (Firebase, etc.)
  • Contact form messages are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your information, including:

  • Requesting access to your data
  • Requesting deletion of your data
  • Deleting all local data by uninstalling the App
  • Opting out of analytics by disabling usage data sharing in your device settings

You can exercise these rights by contacting us.

Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

DMV IQ is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any state DMV, DPS, BMV, or government agency. This app is an independent study tool. For official information, contact your local DMV office.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
