Clear space and size

Keep free space around the logo equal to the height of one cell. The wordmark should be at least 80 px wide on screen, the stacked logo at least 64 px wide, and the mark at least 16 px.

Using the name and logo

DoWrite the company as Oddly Sunny, two words. Use the supplied files unchanged, on backgrounds with enough contrast.
Don'tStretch, rotate, recolor or outline the logo, add effects, or rearrange the cells. Don't write OddlySunny or Oddly sunny.
Product namesOur apps keep their own names and icons, for example Aviator IQ and Citizen Pass. Don't combine them with this logo in a new lockup.
