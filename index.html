Verification Pipeline

Smart enough to teach, disciplined enough to trust.

Generative models make study sessions interactive, but unconstrained LLMs cannot be trusted with regulatory certification. We pair adaptive practice with a four-part audit that checks each question's source, its answer key and its wrong answers before it reaches learners.

Direct Source Grounding

Every question carries a word-for-word quote from the official manual or regulation it came from. No source, no question.

Independent Blind Check

A second model answers questions without seeing the key. When it disagrees, the question goes back for review.

Official Exam Blueprint

Question counts, time limits and pass marks match the real exam, and topic coverage is checked against the published outline.

Giveaway Detection

We test banks for answer patterns, like the right answer sitting in one position or always being the longest, and fix them so scores reflect knowledge.
