Verification Pipeline

Smart enough to teach, disciplined enough to trust.

Generative models make study sessions interactive, but unconstrained LLMs cannot be trusted with regulatory certification. We pair adaptive algorithms with an automated four-stage audit pipeline that validates source authority, distractor logic, and psychometric fidelity before scenarios reach learners.

Direct Statutory Grounding

Every module binds deterministically to current official manuals and statutes. Scenarios lacking primary authority citations are automatically rejected at ingestion.

Multi-Model Blind Validation

Independent secondary models solve curriculum content blind without answer keys, systematically flagging and eliminating ambiguous distractor logic.

Agency Blueprint Calibration

Topic distributions, scoring thresholds, and cognitive pacing are programmatically calibrated to match official state and federal testing rubrics.

Cognitive Distractor Engineering

Distractor options reflect verified human misconception models rather than superficial formatting giveaways, ensuring scores reflect functional mastery.
