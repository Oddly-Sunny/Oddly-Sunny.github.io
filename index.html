The Oddly Sunny Method and responsible AI

A better way to build learning

Our proprietary AI method turns official sources into modern learning. AI helps us build it, and the sources always have the last word.

Grounded in official sources

Every question is drafted from official public material and carries a verbatim quote from its source. If we can't source it, we don't ship it.

Built for real exam conditions

Blueprints, question counts, timing, pass marks and figures are modeled on the official exam, so practice feels like test day.

Checked independently

A second, independent AI review answers every question blind. If it disagrees with the key, the question goes back, not out.

Official answers stand

We never overrule an official answer key. If one looks outdated or disputed, we flag it instead of guessing.

Honest about limits

Our scores show how prepared you are, not a guarantee. We tell you where you stand, even when it isn't flattering.

Always current

Source monitors watch for new editions and rule changes, so the practice updates when the exam does.

Personal by design

Learning that feels personal

AI doesn't just write the practice. It adapts to the person using it.

Adaptive learning paths

The next question depends on what you know and what you don't. Study at your own pace, with weak spots surfaced first and the right resources one tap away.

Readiness insights

See where you stand, which topics are most likely to trip you up, and when you're ready to book the test. No guessing, no false confidence.

Learning that feels like play

Games, challenges and quick rounds turn practice into something you want to come back to, instead of something you put off.
