Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains how Oddly Sunny LLC (“Oddly Sunny”, “we”, “us”) handles information on the website at oddlysunny.com (the “Site”). Oddly Sunny LLC is a single-member limited liability company registered in New Jersey, United States. Our products and apps have their own privacy policies; this one covers the oddlysunny.com marketing site only.

What we collect

  • Analytics. With your consent (see Cookies), we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the Site is used, such as pages viewed, device and browser type, and a general (city-level) location inferred from your IP address. This data is pseudonymous and is not used to identify you.
  • Email. If you email us (for example at hello@oddlysunny.com), we keep your message and contact details to respond. Mail is delivered through Cloudflare Email Routing.
  • Server and security logs. Our hosting and CDN providers (GitHub Pages, fronted by Cloudflare) automatically record standard technical logs such as IP address, request time, and user agent to operate the Site securely.

The Site has no accounts, sign-in, or payments, so we do not collect names, passwords, or payment details here.

Cookies

Analytics uses Google Consent Mode v2 and defaults to denied: no analytics cookies are set until you agree. Where consent is required (the EU/EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, plus any visitor whose country we cannot determine) analytics stays off until you accept. Elsewhere analytics is on by default and you can opt out at any time. We always honor the Global Privacy Control browser signal as an opt-out. You can change your choice any time on the Cookie settings page.

How we use it

To operate and secure the Site, understand how it is used, and respond to messages you send us. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use advertising, marketing, or social-tracking cookies of any kind.

Retention

Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months, then deleted. Server and security logs are kept by our providers for their standard short-retention periods (typically 30 to 90 days). Emails are kept until your inquiry is resolved plus a reasonable archival period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. If you are in California, you may request access to or deletion of your personal information and are entitled not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights. To exercise any right, email us at hello@oddlysunny.com. You can turn analytics off any time on the Cookie settings page or by clearing your browser storage for this site.
